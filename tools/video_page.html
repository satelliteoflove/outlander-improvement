What the video shows

{{shows}}

Frames per second counts how often the game puts a new picture on show, over the last second. Busy is the share of each CPU’s time spent outside a wait loop, and the graph is its last ten seconds.

The game still takes one step of its logic about every eight vblanks, as the original does, so it plays at the same speed. The frames in between are drawn from where things are heading.
